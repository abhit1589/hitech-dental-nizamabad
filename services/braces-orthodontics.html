What are Braces & Orthodontics?

Orthodontic treatment uses braces, aligners, or other appliances to correct misaligned teeth and bite problems. Braces gradually move teeth into their proper positions over time, improving both aesthetics and function. At Hitech Dental Clinic, we offer multiple orthodontic options including traditional metal braces, ceramic (tooth-colored) braces, and clear aligners to suit your lifestyle and preferences.

Who Needs Braces?

Orthodontic treatment can help if you have:

  • Crooked or crowded teeth — teeth that overlap or twist
  • Gaps between teeth — spaces that affect appearance or function
  • Overbite or underbite — upper or lower teeth protruding excessively
  • Crossbite — upper teeth bite inside lower teeth
  • Open bite — front teeth don't meet when biting
  • Jaw alignment issues — problems with chewing or speech

Types of Braces We Offer

  • Metal Braces — traditional, durable, and most cost-effective option
  • Ceramic Braces — tooth-colored brackets that blend with your teeth
  • Clear Aligners — removable transparent trays for discreet treatment

What to Expect During Treatment

1

Consultation & Records

Comprehensive exam, X-rays, photos, and impressions to plan your treatment.

2

Braces Placement

Brackets and wires are bonded to your teeth. This appointment takes 1-2 hours.

3

Regular Adjustments

Monthly visits to adjust wires and monitor progress. Each visit takes 15-30 minutes.

4

Treatment Completion

After 12-24 months (average), braces are removed and teeth are polished.

5

Retention Phase

Wear retainers to maintain your new smile and prevent teeth from shifting back.

Aftercare & Oral Hygiene with Braces

  • Brush after every meal — use a soft toothbrush and brush around brackets carefully
  • Floss daily — use floss threaders or orthodontic floss to clean between teeth
  • Avoid hard, sticky foods — no popcorn, hard candy, chewing gum, or sticky caramels
  • Wear elastics as directed — if prescribed, wear rubber bands consistently
  • Attend all appointments — regular adjustments are crucial for treatment success
  • Use orthodontic wax — if brackets cause irritation, apply wax for comfort

Frequently Asked Questions

Do braces hurt?

Placing braces is painless, though you may feel pressure. After adjustments, teeth may feel sore for 2-3 days, easily managed with over-the-counter pain relievers. Discomfort is temporary and decreases with each adjustment.

How long does treatment take?

Most patients wear braces for 12-24 months, though complex cases may take longer. Treatment duration depends on the severity of misalignment and how well you follow care instructions.

Am I too old for braces?

No! Orthodontic treatment works for patients of all ages. Many adults successfully get braces or clear aligners to improve their smiles. It's never too late to straighten your teeth.

Can I play sports with braces?

Yes, but wear a mouthguard to protect your teeth and braces during contact sports. We can provide a custom orthodontic mouthguard for maximum protection.

What foods should I avoid with braces?

Avoid hard foods (nuts, hard candy, ice), sticky foods (caramel, gum, taffy), and foods you bite into with front teeth (whole apples, corn on the cob). Cut these foods into small pieces instead.
