Crowns & Bridges

Restore damaged teeth and replace missing teeth with natural-looking restorations

What is Crowns & Bridges?

Dental crowns are custom-made caps that cover damaged or weakened teeth, restoring their shape, strength, and appearance. Dental bridges replace one or more missing teeth by anchoring artificial teeth to adjacent natural teeth. Both are permanent solutions that look and function like natural teeth.

Who Needs This Service?

  • Large cavities or extensive decay
  • Cracked or broken teeth
  • Root canal-treated teeth needing protection
  • Missing one or more teeth
  • Severely worn or discolored teeth
  • Desire to improve tooth appearance

What to Expect

1

Examination & X-rays

We assess the tooth and surrounding area to plan treatment.

2

Tooth Preparation

The tooth is reshaped to accommodate the crown, or adjacent teeth are prepared for a bridge.

3

Impressions

Detailed molds of your teeth are taken to create custom restorations.

4

Temporary Crown/Bridge

A temporary restoration protects the tooth while the permanent one is made.

5

Permanent Placement

After 1-2 weeks, the custom crown or bridge is cemented into place.

6

Final Adjustments

We check bite and comfort, making any necessary adjustments.

Aftercare Tips

  • Avoid hard or sticky foods for the first 24 hours
  • Brush and floss around the crown/bridge daily
  • Use a floss threader or water flosser for bridges
  • Attend regular dental checkups
  • Avoid chewing ice or hard objects
  • Report any discomfort or looseness immediately

Frequently Asked Questions

What materials are used for crowns?

We offer porcelain (all-ceramic), zirconia, and metal-ceramic crowns. Porcelain and zirconia provide the best aesthetics for front teeth, while metal-ceramic offers extra strength for back teeth.

How long does the procedure take?

The process typically requires two appointments over 2-3 weeks. The first visit involves preparation and impressions (60-90 minutes), and the second visit is for placement (30-60 minutes).

Are crowns and bridges painful?

No. The procedure is performed under local anesthesia, so you won't feel discomfort. Some mild sensitivity after placement is normal and temporary.

Can a crown fall off?

With proper care, crowns are very secure. However, if the cement weakens over time or the underlying tooth decays, a crown may loosen. Regular checkups help prevent this.

What's the difference between a crown and a bridge?

A crown covers a single damaged tooth, while a bridge replaces one or more missing teeth by attaching artificial teeth to crowns on adjacent natural teeth.
