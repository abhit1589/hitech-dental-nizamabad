What is Teeth Cleaning & Scaling?

Professional teeth cleaning, also known as dental scaling or prophylaxis, is a preventive dental procedure that removes plaque, tartar (calculus), and surface stains from your teeth. Unlike regular brushing and flossing at home, professional cleaning uses specialized instruments to clean areas that are difficult to reach and remove hardened deposits that cannot be removed with a toothbrush.

At Hitech Dental Clinic, we use ultrasonic scalers and hand instruments to provide thorough, comfortable cleaning sessions that help maintain your oral health and prevent gum disease.

Who Needs Professional Teeth Cleaning?

Everyone benefits from regular professional teeth cleaning, typically every 6 months. You especially need cleaning if you notice:

  • Visible tartar buildup — yellow or brown deposits on teeth, especially near the gum line
  • Bleeding gums — gums that bleed when brushing or flossing
  • Bad breath — persistent bad breath despite regular brushing
  • Swollen or tender gums — signs of gingivitis or gum inflammation
  • Surface stains — discoloration from coffee, tea, tobacco, or food
  • Routine maintenance — even with excellent home care, professional cleaning is essential

What to Expect During Your Cleaning

1

Oral Examination

We check your teeth and gums for signs of cavities, gum disease, or other concerns before starting the cleaning.

2

Plaque & Tartar Removal

Using ultrasonic scalers and hand instruments, we gently remove plaque and tartar deposits from tooth surfaces and below the gum line.

3

Polishing

Teeth are polished with a special paste to remove surface stains and create a smooth finish that resists plaque buildup.

4

Flossing

Professional flossing removes any remaining debris between teeth and under the gum line.

5

Fluoride Treatment (Optional)

A fluoride varnish may be applied to strengthen enamel and provide extra protection against cavities.

Aftercare Tips

  • Brush twice daily — use a soft-bristled toothbrush and fluoride toothpaste
  • Floss daily — clean between teeth where your toothbrush can't reach
  • Use mouthwash — an antimicrobial rinse helps reduce bacteria
  • Maintain regular checkups — visit every 6 months for cleaning and examination
  • Limit staining foods — reduce coffee, tea, and tobacco use between cleanings

Frequently Asked Questions

How often should I get my teeth cleaned?

Most people should have professional cleaning every 6 months. If you have gum disease, a history of cavities, or other risk factors, your dentist may recommend cleanings every 3-4 months.

Is teeth cleaning painful?

Most patients experience little to no discomfort during routine cleaning. If you have sensitive teeth or significant tartar buildup, you may feel slight sensitivity, but the procedure is generally well-tolerated. Topical numbing gel can be applied if needed.

How long does a cleaning appointment take?

A routine cleaning appointment typically takes 30-45 minutes, depending on the amount of buildup and the condition of your gums.

Will my gums bleed during cleaning?

If your gums are inflamed or you have gingivitis, some bleeding during cleaning is normal. This is actually a sign that you needed the cleaning. With regular cleanings and improved home care, bleeding should decrease over time.

Can teeth cleaning remove all stains?

Professional cleaning removes surface stains caused by food, drinks, and tobacco. However, it does not change the natural color of your teeth or remove deep intrinsic stains. For more dramatic whitening, consider our teeth whitening services.
