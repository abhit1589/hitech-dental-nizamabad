Pediatric Dentistry

Gentle, child-friendly dental care for healthy growing smiles

What is Pediatric Dentistry?

Pediatric dentistry focuses on oral health care for infants, children, and teenagers. Our M.D.S pediatric dentist creates a positive, fear-free environment where children feel comfortable and safe. We provide preventive care, early intervention, and education to establish healthy dental habits that last a lifetime.

Who Needs This Service?

  • Regular checkups for children (starting from age 1)
  • Cavities or tooth decay in baby teeth or permanent teeth
  • Fluoride treatments and dental sealants
  • Habit counseling (thumb sucking, pacifier use)
  • Dental injuries from falls or sports
  • Orthodontic evaluation for growing children

Aftercare Tips

  • Brush twice daily with fluoride toothpaste (pea-sized amount for kids)
  • Floss once daily once teeth touch each other
  • Limit sugary snacks and drinks between meals
  • Visit the dentist every 6 months
  • Use a mouthguard for sports activities
  • Replace toothbrush every 3 months

Frequently Asked Questions

When should my child first visit the dentist?

The first dental visit should occur by age 1 or within 6 months of the first tooth erupting. Early visits help children become comfortable with the dentist and catch problems before they become serious.

Are baby teeth really important?

Yes! Baby teeth hold space for permanent teeth, help with proper speech development, and allow normal chewing and nutrition. Losing baby teeth too early can cause crowding and orthodontic problems.

How can I prevent cavities in my child?

Brush twice daily with fluoride toothpaste, limit sugary foods and drinks, have regular dental checkups, apply dental sealants on molars, and ensure your child drinks fluoridated water.

What if my child is afraid of the dentist?

Our pediatric dentist is specially trained in child behavior management. We use positive reinforcement, tell-show-do techniques, and create a welcoming environment. Most children overcome dental anxiety with gentle, patient care.

Do children need dental sealants?

Yes, sealants are highly recommended for permanent molars (back teeth). They provide a protective barrier against cavities in the deep grooves where brushing often misses, reducing cavity risk by up to 80%.
