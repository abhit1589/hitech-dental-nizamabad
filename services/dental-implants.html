Dental Implants

Permanent tooth replacement that looks, feels, and functions like natural teeth

What are Dental Implants?

Dental implants are titanium posts surgically placed into your jawbone to replace missing teeth. They act as artificial tooth roots, providing a strong foundation for fixed or removable replacement teeth that match your natural teeth. Unlike dentures or bridges, implants don't slip or cause bone deterioration, and they don't require altering adjacent healthy teeth.

At Hitech Dental Clinic, Dr. Krishna Teja Kommineni, our expert Oral & Maxillofacial Surgeon and Implantologist, uses advanced techniques to provide successful, long-lasting implant solutions.

Who Needs Dental Implants?

Dental implants are suitable for most adults who have lost one or more teeth. You may be a good candidate if you have:

  • One or more missing teeth — from injury, decay, or extraction
  • Sufficient jawbone density — to support the implant (bone grafting available if needed)
  • Healthy gums — free from active periodontal disease
  • Good overall health — able to undergo minor surgery
  • Commitment to oral hygiene — willing to maintain excellent home care
  • Desire for permanent solution — looking for stability better than dentures

What to Expect: Dental Implant Procedure

1

Consultation & Planning

Comprehensive examination, 3D imaging, and treatment planning to assess bone quality and determine the best implant placement.

2

Implant Placement

Under local anesthesia, the titanium implant is surgically placed into the jawbone. This minor surgical procedure takes about 1-2 hours per implant.

3

Healing Period (Osseointegration)

Over 3-6 months, the implant fuses with the jawbone in a process called osseointegration, creating a stable foundation.

4

Abutment Placement

A small connector piece (abutment) is attached to the implant to hold the replacement tooth.

5

Crown Placement

A custom-made dental crown is attached to the abutment, completing your new permanent tooth.

Aftercare Tips

  • Follow post-op instructions — take prescribed medications and follow all care guidelines
  • Soft diet initially — stick to soft foods for the first few days after surgery
  • Avoid smoking — smoking significantly increases implant failure risk
  • Maintain oral hygiene — brush, floss, and use antimicrobial rinse as directed
  • Attend follow-ups — keep all scheduled appointments to monitor healing
  • Avoid excessive pressure — don't chew on the implant site during healing

Frequently Asked Questions

Are dental implants painful?

The procedure is performed under local anesthesia, so you won't feel pain during surgery. Most patients report that discomfort after implant placement is minimal and easily managed with over-the-counter pain relievers. Any soreness typically subsides within a few days.

How long do dental implants last?

With proper care and maintenance, dental implants can last 20-30 years or even a lifetime. The crown may need replacement after 10-15 years due to normal wear, but the implant itself is designed to be permanent.

What is the success rate of dental implants?

Dental implants have a success rate of approximately 95-98% when placed by an experienced implantologist. Success depends on factors like oral hygiene, overall health, and following aftercare instructions.

Can I get implants if I have bone loss?

Yes. If you don't have sufficient jawbone density, bone grafting procedures can build up the bone to support implants. Dr. Krishna Teja can evaluate your case and recommend the best approach.

How much do dental implants cost?

The cost depends on the number of implants needed, whether bone grafting is required, and the type of restoration (crown, bridge, or denture). We provide transparent pricing during your consultation. Contact us for a personalized treatment plan and cost estimate.

Can I replace all my teeth with implants?

Yes. Full-mouth rehabilitation using implants is possible. Options include implant-supported dentures (snap-on dentures) or a full arch of individual implant-supported crowns. Dr. Krishna Teja specializes in full-mouth reconstruction.
