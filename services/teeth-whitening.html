Teeth Whitening

Get a brighter, whiter smile in just one visit

What is Teeth Whitening?

Professional teeth whitening is a cosmetic dental procedure that uses safe bleaching agents to remove stains and discoloration from teeth. Our in-office laser whitening treatment can brighten your smile by several shades in a single appointment, while take-home kits offer gradual whitening at your convenience.

Who Needs This Service?

  • Stained teeth from coffee, tea, wine, or tobacco
  • Yellow or discolored teeth from aging
  • Special occasions (weddings, interviews, events)
  • Desire for a brighter,confident smile
  • Good oral health with no active decay

Aftercare Tips

  • Avoid staining foods and drinks for 48 hours (coffee, tea, red wine, berries)
  • Use a straw when drinking colored beverages
  • Brush with whitening toothpaste to maintain results
  • Avoid tobacco products
  • Touch up every 6-12 months as needed
  • Maintain regular dental cleanings

Frequently Asked Questions

Is teeth whitening safe?

Yes. Professional teeth whitening under dental supervision is safe and effective. We use dentist-approved products that have been tested for safety. Some patients may experience temporary tooth sensitivity, which resolves within a day or two.

How long do whitening results last?

Results typically last 1-3 years depending on your habits. Avoiding staining foods/drinks and tobacco, plus regular brushing and touchups, helps maintain your bright smile longer.

Will whitening work on all teeth?

Whitening works best on natural tooth enamel and may not be effective on crowns, veneers, or fillings. If you have restorations in visible areas, we can discuss replacement options to match your new shade.

Does whitening damage enamel?

No. Professional whitening does not damage tooth enamel when performed correctly. The process only removes stains and does not alter tooth structure.

Can I whiten my teeth at home?

We offer professional take-home whitening kits with custom trays for gradual whitening. These are safer and more effective than over-the-counter products.
